INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The jump-diffusion model separates normal price dynamics (diffusion)
from extreme price spikes (Poisson jumps).  Key findings:
  λ_dis > 0: discharge predicts (but does not cause) jumps
    Batteries position strategically before anticipated spikes
    The causal dampening operates via β_dis < 0 (drift channel)
  ✓ μ_J > 0: jumps are upward price spikes (as expected)
  ✓ σ_J >> σ: jump variance dominates diffusion variance
